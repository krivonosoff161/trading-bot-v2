# Geometry Research - Public Boundary Notice

Status: **ARCHIVE / SANITIZED**. Updated 2026-07-10.

Historical geometry experiments, charts, trade-level paths, and parameter
comparisons are private research artifacts. They are intentionally not published
in this repository because they can expose raw calculations, strategy edge, or
misleading historical performance impressions.

The public method is retained:

1. reconstruct a post-entry price path from locally available data;
2. distinguish a signal from a filled paper observation;
3. measure adverse and favorable movement after the fill;
4. validate any proposed geometry change through the independent validation and
   paper-evidence path;
5. publish only a sanitized methodological conclusion, never raw trade paths,
   parameter sweeps, rankings, or performance claims.

See [Storage Boundaries](../storage_boundaries.md) and
[Document Catalog](../document-catalog.md) for the public/private policy.
